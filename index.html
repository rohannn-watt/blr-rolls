Our Story

Fast. Flavour-packed. Unapologetically loaded.

BLR Rolls is a street-style roll counter in Jayanti Nagar, Bangalore, serving up fresh-off-the-tawa chicken, egg, paneer and signature rolls in a dark, moody, no-frills setup built for people who want big flavour, fast. Every roll is made to order, loaded with fillings, and finished with our house sauces.

Run by Pranav Sirigiri, BLR Rolls keeps it simple: hot griddles, honest ingredients, and a menu built for cravings at any hour of the day.
